The concert

Samba Rosa

A rare musical complicity led by the magnetism of SELMAH and the virtuoso breath of harmonica player Giuseppe Milici, under the musical direction of Luigi Rana (multi-platinum, Europe and Asia). The project reveals a sunlit, rhythmic Italy, deeply connected to the heart of Brazil, and celebrates fifty years of the album «La voglia e la pazzia, l'incoscienza e l'allegria» by Ornella Vanoni, Toquinho and Vinicius de Moraes.

On stage (sextet): SELMAH (vocals, percussion) · Giuseppe Milici (harmonica) · Luigi Rana (musical direction, classical guitar) · Trio Unisono (bass, piano and drums).
